# 00 — Adversarial fact-check (2026-11-01)

Each research file's load-bearing claims were re-opened against primary sources by an independent refute-shaped checker. **Where a verdict below disagrees with a research file, the verdict wins.** `06-roadmap.md` was written after these verdicts and already applies them.


## market

**Impact:** Most of the load-bearing market facts still hold: the GoCardless closure and the Enable Banking alternative, US premium pricing of $99–109/yr, the French incumbents' pricing and gating, Cashew's traction, and FinanceKit being unavailable in France. The '3 clics' legal lever is confirmed on Légifrance and can be upgraded to Verified. Add the nuances: compliance deadline 1 Sept 2023, consumer contracts sold online only, and no literal click count. The 'no French premium method app' claim is refuted in its absolute form, because Finzee (a small, manual, envelope/reste-à-vivre app with French localisation) and Plan & Multiply exist. Neither has scale, so the positioning gap survives but must be reworded from 'none exists' to 'only tiny, low-traction entrants; no scaled brand'. Treat Finzee's 'reste à vivre' envelopes as direct prior art for the safe-to-spend idea, which weakens that idea as a differentiator unless it is backed by the recurrence engine and the bill/contract lifecycle. Keep FiDA out of any near-term plan. No change to the core recommendation: manual and smart input first, optional bank sync behind an abstraction.

## compliance

**Impact:** None of the load-bearing claims was refuted, and the compliance recommendations stand. Store fees, the Play 12-tester/14-day rule, the SMS exception, GoCardless being closed, the sqlite3 3.x encryption path, FSL and the CNIL list all hold as of 2026-11-01.
